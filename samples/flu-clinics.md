# County Flu Vaccination Program 2025

## Eligibility

All county residents aged 6 months and older are eligible for a free seasonal flu vaccine.
No insurance is required. Bring a photo ID or proof of county residence.

## Locations and hours

Vaccines are offered at the Central, Northside, and Riverside public health clinics.
Walk-ins are accepted Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM, from October 1 through March 31.

## High-dose vaccines

Residents aged 65 and older may request a high-dose or adjuvanted vaccine at any clinic.
